PRIVACY POLICY – SkyView Atlas

Last updated: 21 November 2026

SkyView Atlas ("the app", "we", "us") is an astronomy app that shows the night sky for your location. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. We do not require an account, and we do not run our own servers that collect your data.

1. INFORMATION THE APP USES

a) Location (approximate or precise)

The app uses your device location, only while the app is open, to work out which part of the sky is above you (sunrise, planet positions, satellite passes, etc.). The app does not request background location. Your location is stored only on your device. If you prefer, you can type a location manually in Settings and deny the location permission.
To show a place name (for example a city) the app may ask the Android system geocoder, which is provided by Google and may send your coordinates to Google to look up the name. We do not receive that data.

b) Camera

The camera is used only in the AR mode to show the live camera image behind the sky overlay. Camera images are processed on your device. They are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere, unless you press the screenshot button – in which case the picture is saved to your own gallery and shared only if you choose to share it.

c) Motion and compass sensors

Orientation sensors (compass, accelerometer, gyroscope) are used on your device to point the sky view where your phone is pointing. Sensor data never leaves your device.

d) Data stored on your device

Your settings, favourite objects, saved location and downloaded satellite orbit data are stored locally on your device. You can remove all of it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

e) Screenshots

Screenshots are created only when you tap the capture button. They are saved to your gallery (Pictures/SkyAtlas). If you enable the option, the date, time and location are written onto the image. You decide whether to share the image.

2. INFORMATION SENT OVER THE INTERNET

The app works offline for the sky map, planets, Sun, Moon and catalogues. Optional features contact the following services:
• CelesTrak (celestrak.org) – to download satellite orbit data. The request contains no personal information, but the server sees your IP address like any website.
• Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org) – to load planet pictures. The server sees your IP address.
• Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform – to show ads and, where required, collect your consent (see section 3).
• Google Play (Billing and Play services) – to process the optional Premium purchase and to provide location services. Payments are handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details.

3. ADVERTISING

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and other device and usage information to show ads and measure their performance, in line with Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Where the law requires (for example in the EEA, UK and some other regions) the app shows a consent form before requesting ads, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Privacy & about > "Ad privacy choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Ads are never shown on the live sky map or AR camera screens. The Premium purchase removes ads.

4. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use the information described above only to provide the app's features. We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties except the services listed above that are needed to provide ads, purchases, maps of the sky data and location services.

5. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

We do not store your personal data on any server of ours. Data held on your device can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. For data held by Google (for example advertising data), please use your Google account's privacy controls.

6. CHILDREN

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

7. SECURITY

Network requests are made over encrypted HTTPS connections. Data stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your personal data on our servers, most of these actions can be done directly on your device or in your Google account settings. You can also contact us using the details below.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
